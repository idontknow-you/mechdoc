Mandatory Engineering Advisory • OSHA 1910.147 Compliant

Human-in-the-Loop Diagnostic Protocol

MechDoctor is configured strictly as a supervisory diagnostic aid and advisory system. Automated fault recommendations and AI contextual suggestions do not supersede physical lockout/tagout (LOTO) protocols, onsite thermographic verifications, or certified plant engineer approvals. All critical corrective procedures (bearing replacement, coupling realignments, and internal valve servicing) must be authorized by licensed reliability personnel prior to execution.
