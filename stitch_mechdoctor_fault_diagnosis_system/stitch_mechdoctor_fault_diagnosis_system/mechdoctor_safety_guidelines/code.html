Five Core Plant Safety Pillars

Zero-Harm Diagnostic Directives
Pillar 01
power_off

Complete Machinery Isolation

Zero mechanical or kinetic motion must be confirmed prior to panel entry or guard separation.

block Never inspect or touch moving couplings, belts, impellers, or drivelines while energized.
valve Disconnect mechanical drives, hydraulic accumulators, and open pneumatic bleed lines.
motion_photos_off Verify zero residual kinetic flywheel or rotational momentum before guard disassembly.
Status: Critical Mandate 100% De-Energize
Pillar 02
lock

Lockout / Tagout (LOTO) Compliance

Execution of OSHA 29 CFR 1910.147 hazardous energy isolation protocols.

check_circle Apply individual master safety padlock and red Danger Tag at the primary breaker.
group_work Group lockout hasps required when multi-craft teams inspect a common powertrain.
play_disabled Mandatory "Try-Out": Attempt manual start sequence to verify absolute zero energy state.

Pillar 04
menu_book

Adherence to OEM Specifications

Component tolerances must strictly reflect manufacturer technical data plates.

straighten Verify internal bearing clearances (C2/CN/C3), cold alignment specs, and torque ratings.
speed Never bypass maximum rated motor drive RPM or thermal operational thresholds.
oil_barrel Use verified synthetic or mineral formulations; prevent catastrophic polyurea/lithium thickener clash.
Reference Factory Manuals
Pillar 05
support_agent

Qualified Technical Assistance & Escalation Boundaries

Strict operational boundaries requiring immediate work stoppage and specialist certified intervention.

bolt Electrical Breakdown

Immediate transfer to licensed industrial master electricians when Megger insulation readings drop below < 1 MΩ.

air Confined Space

Never enter ducting, silos, or machine wells without four-gas atmospheric air monitoring, valid permits, and attendant.

water_drop Hydraulic Jet Risk

Pinhole hydraulic leaks carry severe fluid injection hazards. NEVER use bare or gloved hands to detect micro-leaks; use cardboard/wood.

Non-Contact Diagnostic Protocol

Whenever operational vibration telemetry or thermal imaging is recorded while machinery is running, technicians must maintain minimum clearance boundaries:

  • Maintain ≥ 1.0 m standoff distance using optical tachometers.
  • Secure sensor cables using tie-wraps to prevent shaft entanglement.
  • Stand completely clear of rotational plane during variable speed ramp-ups.
